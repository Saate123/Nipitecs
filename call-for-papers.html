Call for Papers - NIPITECS 2025

The Technical Planning Committee for the 2025 edition of the NIPITECS Conference has officially opened the window for Paper Submissions.

Show your expertise and knowledge before 100s of industry leaders and peers at NIPITECS, the top International Pipeline Security and Technology Conference, this November

NIPITECS is a platform to experience cutting-edge presentations, workshops, and networking to address key challenges in pipeline security. Don't miss the chance to shape the future of pipeline security.

THEME: BUILDING ROBUST PIPELINE SYSTEMS FOR THE DECADE OF GAS & BEYOND...

Sub Themes:

  • Engineering & Technology: Advanced materials, corrosion control, pipeline integrity, AI-driven monitoring, and subsea pipeline solutions.
  • Economics & Finance: Innovative financing, market dynamics, pricing, and risk management.
  • Policy & Regulation: Cross-border agreements, safety/environmental regulations, and energy geopolitics.
  • Sustainability & Environment: Reducing impact, hydrogen/carbon pipeline adaptation, and community engagement.

INSTRUCTIONS FOR PREPARATION OF MANUSCRIPTS

  • ABSTRACT: It must not exceed a single paragraph of 160 words. Line spacing: Single
  • MEDIUM: Manuscripts accepted must be submitted in electronic form as a single file. Format: MS Word or MS PowerPoint
  • ILLUSTRATIONS AND GRAPHICS: All drawings shall be presented electronically with software compatible with Microsoft Office Suite (e.g. MS PowerPoint, Visio, etc) and integrated seamlessly into the MS Word or MS PowerPoint document
  • EQUATIONS: Mathematical equations shall be constructed within the document using the Microsoft Equation Editor included with the MS Office Suite. Equations should be numbered.
  • TABLES AND FIGURES: All tables should be labelled at the top with the word Table followed by the sequential numerical letter. All figures should be labelled at the bottom with the word "Figure" followed by sequential numerical letter
  • FONT: The font should be "Times New Roman" (serif) or "Arial" (sans serif), with font sizes 14-point (14 pts) for the Title, 13-point (13 pts) for sub-headings and 12-point for body text.. Line spacing: 1.5
  • NUMBER OF PAGES: Number of pages should not exceed twelve (12)
  • LAYOUT: Paper size: ISO A4; Margins: Top, Right, Bottom, Left Margins: 2cm
  • AFFILIATION ADDRESS / KEYWORDS: Name of Institution, E-mail address and Telephone number of the corresponding author(s) should be provided Provide no more than six (6) keywords

Important Dates:

  • END OF SUBMISSION OF ABSRTACT: 15 October 2025
  • NOTIFICATION OF ACCEPTANCE OF THE ABSTRACT: ON OR BEFORE 20 October 2025
  • SUBMISSION OF FULL MANUSCRIPT: 24 October 2025
  • SUBMISSION OF CORRECTED FULL MANUSCRIPT: 30 October 2025
  • NOTIFICATION OF ACCEPTANCE OF FULL MANUSCRIPT: 31 October 2025

About

NIPITECS was established in 2013 through a collaboration between the Pipeline Professionals Association of Nigeria (PLAN) and Energy Corporate Africa (ECA), a renowned US-based organizer of energy events. The goal was simple yet powerful: to position Nigeria and Sub-Saharan Africa on the global map for pipeline infrastructure, technology, and security investment.
